Pichardo Marcano

Instituto de Astronomía
Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México

I currently search for exoplanets around brown dwarfs using SAINT-EX data. I also study white dwarf binaries, variable stars, and compact objects in globular clusters, with interests in accretion and gravitational waves.
